Why Ikon Technologies

Built by people who ran stores

Most telematics is adapted for dealerships. Ikon Technologies was designed inside them, which is why it maps to how a store actually runs rather than to how a tracking vendor thinks it does.

  • One program, not four vendors

    Lot, recovery, F&I and retention run off the same device and the same data. Nothing to reconcile between systems.

  • No upfront device cost

    Hardware and install carry no capital outlay. The program earns when the store does.

    A dealer-branded app keeps your name on the customer's phone through the whole ownership cycle, and brings them back for the next vehicle.

    Recovery is a service, coordinated with law enforcement, not a dashboard you're left to work alone at 2am.
