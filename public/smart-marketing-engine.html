How the Smart Marketing AI-Engine works

Ikon's engine pulls what we know about every customer into one place, lets a dealer ask for an audience in plain words, writes a message for each person in it and sends it on the channel they actually use. Below, the same pipeline built as a machine.

  1. Data in. Ikon telematics (location, battery, diagnostic codes), CRM (name, email, mailing address) and DMS (inventory, sales, product and service history) feed one datalake.
  2. Ask. A dealer types a question, like “customers who haven't been in for service in 6 months or 5,000 miles”. The Ikon LLM turns it into a query over the datalake.
  3. Audience. Only the customers that fit go forward; everyone else is left out of this campaign.
  4. Content. AI writes a message for each customer, based on their own vehicle and history.
  5. Send. The API delivers each message on that customer's channel: an offer in the Connect app, a targeted Google or Meta ad, an email, an SMS or a personal AI voice call.
